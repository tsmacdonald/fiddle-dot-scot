Niel Gow turns 300 in March—let's celebrate with workshops, a mini-conference, and a concert!

## Date and Venue

**Saturday 27 March 2027** at [St Cecilia's Hall](https://www.stcecilias.ed.ac.uk/) in the Old Town of Edinburgh.

## Music Workshop

Learn some Niel Gow tunes and what we know of his famous style from fiddler [Tim
Macdonald](https://tsmacdonald.com/) and cellist [Claire Garabedian](https://drclairegarabedian.com/). Harmony players
will get to work with the original bass lines—a much more percussive approach than modern chordal accompaniment!

Open to all musicians on any reasonable instrument as long as they have an interest in the music and play to a basic
standard. Tunes will be taught by ear, but sheet music will be distributed for visual learners.

Everyone is encouraged to try out their new skills by joining the dance band for the following workshop. (Dancing is also
encouraged!)

*Register at [link TBC].*

## Dance Workshop

Learn to dance a Highland Reel, 18th-century style!

Open to all; no prior dance experience of any sort necessary.

## Conference

A conference on any aspect of Niel Gow's life or music, starting at 1.30pm.

*Register at [link TBC] as either a participant or listener.*

### CFP

Submissions are invited for papers (20 minutes + 10 for Q&A), lecture-concerts, roundtable discussions, or 5-minute
lightning talks. Suggested topics include Niel Gow's life or music, their historical contexts, or their subsequent
legacy.

Submit an abstract to <a href="mailto:gow.conference@tsmacdonald.com">gow.conference@tsmacdonald.com</a>.

## Concert

We'll finish with a ceilidh concert featuring some great trad musicians playing their responses to Niel Gow. Book your
ticket at [link TBC]!

*If you would like to be play (and receive a modest fee), please email <a href="mailto:gow.concert@tsmacdonald.com">gow.concert@tsmacdonald.com</a>.*
